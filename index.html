    Cooking on a budget

    • Lentils, beans, rice, and oats are cheap and filling. Half of this page is built on them.
    • Cook double, eat twice. Tonight's dinner is tomorrow's lunch.
    • Frozen vegetables are just as good for you and they don't spoil.
    • Eggs are the cheapest protein you can buy.
    • Chicken thighs and drumsticks are usually cheaper than breast, and harder to dry out.

    Plate it like a restaurant

    • Use a big plate and leave empty space around the food. Less crowded looks fancier.
    • Wipe the rim with a paper towel before you serve.
    • Finish with something fresh: parsley, basil, or a squeeze of lemon.
    • Drizzle olive oil or sprinkle a little flaky salt on top at the end.
    • Light a candle and put the phone away. That's half of fine dining.
